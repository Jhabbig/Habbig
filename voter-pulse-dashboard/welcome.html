Composite mood index

Equal-weight blend of pocketbook (CPI / gas / mortgage / home prices), jobs (UNRATE / real wages / payrolls / disposable income), and sentiment (UMich CSI). 0 = bleak, 100 = good. Every band cutoff is public and unfitted.

Election backtest

We rebuild the mood index for every month since 1978 using only data available at that point, then ask whether it would have called every presidential election from 1980 onward. No retuning — same formula the live gauge uses.

Across America

A 50-state tile-map coloured by current unemployment. Click any state for its 1-year and 4-year deltas. Regional cuts (Northeast / Midwest / South / West) carry the same mood formula with regional inputs.

World jobs — Clark–Fisher arc

Every country plotted on a sector-employment ternary (agriculture / industry / services), with the five Clark–Fisher development stages shaded behind them. Click any country for its full 1991→today sector trajectory.

Polling aggregate

Monthly mean of every public presidential-approval poll and the generic congressional ballot, aggregated from the archived FiveThirtyEight CSV dataset. Cached, charted, comparable.

AI narrative + share cards

A 3-sentence summary regenerated whenever the data moves. Every panel has a public shareable URL that unfurls into a 1200×630 OG image on Twitter, Slack, and Discord.

Tell me when something moves

We'll email you when the composite mood index moves more than 5 points. No marketing, no daily noise.
