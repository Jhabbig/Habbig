Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

  • Account data: email address, username, and a salted password hash. That's the profile.
  • Billing data: handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, never card numbers.
  • Usage data: standard server logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) kept for security and debugging, and rate-limit counters. No third-party analytics, no ad trackers.
  • Waitlist/support: if you join the waitlist or write to support, we keep the email and message you sent us.

Cookies

We set a session cookie to keep you signed in and a CSRF token to protect forms. That's it — no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

What we do with it

Operate the service, process your subscription, respond when you write to us, and send transactional email (password resets, billing confirmations). We do not sell or share your data with advertisers, period.

Third parties

Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy. Dashboard data comes from public APIs and is not linked to your identity.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account exists. Email julian@narve.ai to delete your account and associated data; we honor deletion requests within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax or accounting purposes.

Contact

Data questions: julian@narve.ai. See also our Terms of Service and Impressum.
